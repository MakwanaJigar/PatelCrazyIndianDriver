## Brand & Style
This design system is engineered for fast-paced, high-stress transit environments where ambient sunlight, quick glances, and extreme operational clarity dictate usability. The personality is energetic, commanding, hyper-legible, and resolute. 

Drawing from Modern Industrial High-Contrast and Utilitarian Data-Dense movements, the visual posture avoids decorative fluff in favor of immediate affordances. Surfaces are pure, luminous, and structured, providing an unyielding foundation for hot fiery reds and crisp status indicators. Interaction targets are exaggerated for one-handed operation under vehicle vibration. The emotional outcome for the driver is zero friction, high adrenaline control, unwavering operational clarity, and complete confidence in earnings, routing, and live alerts.

## Colors
The palette leverages radiant light backdrops juxtaposed against dense slate-charcoals to guarantee uncompromised outdoor visibility under direct solar glare.

- **Primary (`#DC2626` / `#E11D48`)**: High-visibility fiery red representing immediate action, core dispatches, urgent calls-to-action, and brand presence. Reserved strictly for primary navigation triggers, accept banners, and key anchors.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A`)**: Saturated emerald green dedicated strictly to affirmative operational states: Active On-Duty status, incoming cash totals, completed fares, and positive incentives.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: High-luminance amber/orange designated for dynamic surge pricing, traffic friction alerts, routing caution, and expiring trip countdown timers.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for radiant, clean backdrops without chromatic fatigue.
  - `Card / Sheet Surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) for crisp foreground distinction.
  - `Muted / Structural Surfaces`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) and `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for nested containers, metric tracks, and card divisions.
- **Typography Neutrals**:
  - `Primary Text`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) providing extreme contrast ratios exceeding 14:1 against pure white.
  - `Secondary Text`: `#1E293B` (Slate 800) for subheads and high-importance data keys.
  - `Tertiary Text`: `#334155` (Slate 700) for contextual timestamps, peripheral metadata, and inactive labels.
- **Structural Outlines**: `#E2E8F0` defining hard, sharp structural perimeters that preserve clarity across low-grade smartphone displays.

## Typography
Typography emphasizes immediate numerical recognition and quick-scan comprehension. 

- **Display & Labels (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, geometric, and assertive. Used for all numbers, monetary balances, speed, turn cues, badges, and button triggers. Tabular lining ensures fluctuating metrics do not cause horizontal layout jitter.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Clean, rational, high x-height grotesque that prevents visual blur under rapid vibration or sunlight flare. Used for street names, navigation instruction sub-steps, customer chat, and settlement breakdowns.
- **Hierarchy Strategy**: Headlines and monetary figures (`metric-huge`, `headline-xl`) establish unmissable focal points. Body weights do not drop below `500` (Medium) to ensure ink traps remain crisp against daylight backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model follows a rigid, compact fluid grid optimized for edge-to-edge mobile interfaces mounted on dashboard phone docks.

- **Mobile Viewport (<640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Crucial operational triggers lock to bottom sheets within the natural thumb zone. Top zones are dedicated to large turn indicators and telemetry metrics.
- **Tablet / Mounted Landscape (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout split into a 5-column navigation map canvas and a 3-column persistent operational dock (trip queues, incoming bids, telemetry).
- **Rhythm**: Compact base units (`0.25rem` / `0.5rem`) minimize unused dead space, keeping high-priority information above the fold without clutter. Vertical stacks rely on `space-md` (`0.75rem`) for tight structural clustering and `space-xl` (`1.5rem`) between unrelated operational cards.

## Elevation & Depth
In bright outdoor light, soft diffuse drop shadows vanish or generate visual haze. Therefore, this design system establishes hierarchy through tonal layering, crisp borders, and punchy, tactile drop offsets.

- **Flat Tonal Base**: The floor rests at `#F8FAFC`. Base floating panels, bottom sheets, and route cards lift via pure `#FFFFFF` fills wrapped in high-definition `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders.
- **Directional Hard Shadows**: For high-priority action cards and floating HUD elements, elevation relies on razor-sharp, low-blur offsets: `0px 4px 0px 0px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. This creates a decisive, mechanical separation from the background map or base canvas.
- **Active / Alert Overlays**: Urgent request overlays and acceptance modals utilize high-contrast borders (`2px solid #DC2626` or `2px solid #16A34A`) combined with a solid dark backing scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`) to cut through map clutter instantly.

## Shapes
Geometry is structured, mechanical, and assertive. A base corner radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`) balances modern ergonomics with high-density utility.

- **Cards and Bottom Sheets**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) on exterior perimeters, providing a friendly yet strictly contained physical metaphor.
- **Buttons and Primary Interactive Badges**: `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) preserves sharp edge definition for quick tap recognition.
- **Status Pills and Turn Chips**: `rounded-full` is restricted strictly to compact status indicators (e.g., "SURGE 2.4x", "ONLINE") and micro tags to contrast distinctly against structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational Button (Accept / Go Online)**: Full-width, minimum height `56px` for effortless palm or thumb tapping. Background `#DC2626` (or `#16A34A` for on-duty toggle), typography `label-lg` in `#FFFFFF`, with a subtle recessed border `inset 0px -2px 0px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`.
- **Secondary Action Button (Navigate / Call)**: `#F1F5F9` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Height `48px`.
- **Swipe-to-Confirm Slider**: High-tactility full-width container (`#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`) with a floating thumb slider (`#16A34A` or `#DC2626`, `rounded-md`) requiring continuous drag gesture to prevent accidental job triggers while driving.

### Cards & Bottom Sheets
- **Ride Request Card**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, `2px solid #DC2626` accent boundary during the request countdown, `rounded-lg` with `1rem` internal padding. Hosts oversized monetary metric (`metric-huge`), surge tag, pickup distance, and a linear decreasing SVG progress bar for the acceptance window.
- **Route / Metric Container**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, containing inner nested tiles styled in `#F8FAFC` to isolate speed, estimated arrival time, and route distance.

### Chips & Badges
- **Surge Chip**: High-visibility amber `#D97706` background with bold `#FFFFFF` text or `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` border and text. Always set in uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Status Pill (On-Duty / Offline)**: `#16A34A` solid fill with animated white beacon dot for active states; `#E2E8F0` fill with `#334155` text when offline.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Address / Destination Input**: Height `52px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #E2E8F0`, resting text `#0F172A` (`body-md`), active focus state jumps directly to `2px solid #0F172A` (or brand `#DC2626`) with zero fuzzy glow. Includes clear high-contrast icon slots (`24px`).

### Lists & Navigation Steps
- **Maneuver Lists**: Edge-to-edge `#FFFFFF` rows divided by razor-thin `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Primary maneuver icons use bold `#0F172A` iconography on `#F1F5F9` circular badges, paired with street names in `headline-sm`.
- **Earnings Breakdown List**: Split items with high-contrast text pairs: left-aligned descriptive label in `body-sm` (`#334155`) and right-aligned tabular currency figure in `label-md` (`#0F172A`).
